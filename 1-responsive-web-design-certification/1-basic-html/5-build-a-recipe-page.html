Rendang & Rawon Double Combo

This monster of an instant ramen recipe requires 2 iconic and distinct Indomie variants, Rendang and Rawon

Ingredients

  • 2.5 glasses of water
  • 1 pack of Indomie Rendang
  • 1 pack of Indomie Rawon
  • 1 pcs of Kerupuk Udang

Instructions

  1. Bring in 2 and a half glass of water into the pot.
  2. Turn on the heat and wait for it to boil.
  3. Open up both packs of the ramen.
  4. Take out all of the seasoning packs into a bowl.
  5. When the water is boiling hot, enter the noodles.
  6. Serve the ramen with Kerupuk Udang
